Open-source drone search and rescue, from the search plan to the rescuer's map. Everything is measured on public data the models never trained on and in ArduPilot's simulator; **it has not yet flown on real hardware.**

### Highlights
- **GPU-trained detectors** (60-120 epochs), scored on the same held-out sets as the first CPU models: AP +22% at sea, +15% on thermal, +32% on land. On four real sea clips, 20 of 24 swimmers raise an alert (was 17). Sea and thermal models ship in `models/` with model cards and SHA-256 checksums.
- **Flight safety:** every plan is checked before upload (5 m under the EU 120 m ceiling, inclusion geofence, take-off and return); the autopilot is set to enforce the same limits and each setting is read back. Both example missions are flown take-off to landing in ArduPilot SITL in CI (14/14 and 48/48 waypoints).
- **Secure ground station:** operator tokens, origin checks, loopback by default, TLS, hash-chained audit log, rate limits, safe map popups with a Content-Security-Policy, tested in a real browser in CI.
- **RGB + thermal to the operator:** camera-scoped IDs, one fused alert per person, non-blocking TAK output.
- **Supply chain:** SHA-pinned, read-only CI checked by zizmor; Dependabot; `SECURITY.md`.

### Known weak spots
- The thermal model labels many people as vehicles above 120 m at night (inside the 115 m flight envelope it finds 91% of people).
- Life rings and vests are almost never detected (AP 0.019).
- No Jetson measurements yet; no field tests yet.

### Assets
